UTEP Sun Bowl Parking Garage Permit (SBG)
PG-1 (Premium) permits allow parking on levels 1 through 5 and in all green zones.Gold PG-1 zone is strictly enforced between 7:00 am and 4:00pm weekdays.
Easy Venue Access: Sun Bowl Stadium, Magoffin Auditorium, Fox Fine Arts Recital Hall, and Wise Family Theatre.
UTEP Schuster Parking Garage (SCG)
PG-2 (Premium) permits allow parking on levels 1 through 5 and in all green zones. Gold PG-2 zone is strictly enforced between 7:00 am and 4:00pm weekdays.
Easy Venue Access: Overflow parking for Sun Bowl Stadium events.
