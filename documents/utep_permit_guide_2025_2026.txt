🛑 Key Rules to Know
Permits are
non-transferable
.
Only one vehicle per permit may be parked on campus at any time.
Employees enrolled in classes may not purchase student permits.
No student parking in Inner Campus 7 AM–5 PM unless ADA-approved.
Hangtags must go on the rearview mirror; adhesive permits on front windshield.
